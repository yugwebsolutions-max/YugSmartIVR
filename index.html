WHAT IS YUG SMART IVR?

One Smart Device to Manage Your Business Calls

YUG Smart IVR is a business communication solution designed to automate and manage incoming and outgoing calls using SIM-based connectivity.

With YUG Smart IVR, businesses can create professional IVR menus, route incoming calls, automate outbound voice campaigns, send SMS and manage manual calls from a centralized system.

Whether you need a compact Single SIM IVR or a high-capacity 8 SIM IVR, you can choose the configuration based on your business requirements.
