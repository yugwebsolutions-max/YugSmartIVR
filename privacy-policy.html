2 100% On-Premise Hardware Data Isolation Guarantee

Unlike third-party cloud telephony services that route private business telephone calls through external third-party servers, Yug Smart IVR is built upon a 100% On-Premise, Localized Architecture:

  • Zero Cloud Audio Interception: All customer phone calls, IVR voice flows, menu navigations, and caller recordings are processed and stored exclusively inside the local flash storage / micro-SD / hard drive of your physical Yug Smart IVR hardware appliance located on your premises.
  • No External Server Transmission: Customer telephone conversation audio and SIM SMS payloads are never streamed, duplicated, or backed up to any cloud server maintained by Yug Web Solutions or external providers.
  • Complete Operational Ownership: Your organization retains 100% exclusive proprietary ownership over all customer call records, caller phone numbers, timestamps, and audio recordings.
🛡️ Zero-Access Privacy Commitment: Yug Web Solutions does not possess backdoors, cloud eavesdropping mechanisms, or remote access to your recorded business phone calls. Your customer database remains confidential within your four walls.
